### Why these clauses matter for renewals (plain-language note for you)

- **Section 3(a)** makes bypassing the licence check an explicit **breach of
  contract** — this is your real protection for self-hosted software, far more
  enforceable against a university than any technical lock.
- **Section 3(c)** stops them sharing the Software with other campuses/institutions.
- **Section 5.2** ties **updates and support to an active licence** — the
  practical reason institutions renew (and it's backed in code by the update
  gating). A lapsed customer keeps their data and running system but stops
  receiving the patches, fixes, and help that keep them current and compliant.
- **Section 10.2** lets you **terminate** for non-payment or for tampering.

These together make renewal the path of least resistance for a legitimate
institutional buyer: it's contractually required to stay licensed, and renewing is
how they keep getting updates and support.
